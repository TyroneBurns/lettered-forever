Hello, I’m Lotti

I’m the founder of Lettered Forever, and mama to three little ones. Embroidery has become my way of slowing down, getting creative, and finding a little time in the week to fill my own cup.

My embroidery journey started when my best friend told me she was pregnant. I knew straight away that I wanted to create something special for her and her little girl – a keepsake, an heirloom, that they could both treasure forever.

From there, it became a little tradition. Whenever a friend or family member announced they were expecting, I would create a personalised embroidered cushion, muslin or babygrow to gift them when their baby arrived.

Friends of friends were then reaching out to me asking me to create something after seeing my designs – framed embroidered fabric, clothing, beaded embroidery, birthday gifts and lots more.

What started as a hobby accidentally turned into a bit of a side gig. So here’s my business page to help me fund the daily pastel de natas I’ve been consuming since moving to Portugal!
